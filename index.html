Who I Am

I'm an engineering student who spends more time in the editor than the lecture hall. What started as curiosity about how websites work turned into a habit of shipping real, working products — from trading platforms to mentorship portals — built end to end with JavaScript and Firebase.

I care about the details most people skip: fast load times, clean auth flows, and interfaces that feel considered rather than assembled.
